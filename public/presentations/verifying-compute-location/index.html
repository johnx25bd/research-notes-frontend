The pipeline.

  • "Location verification" usually blends three different jobs
  • Collect evidence → evaluate evidence → evaluate policy
  • Middle stage neutral + auditable: it neither measures nor decides
  • Anchors at known coordinates probe the attester; nonce challenge
  • Receipt: signed, timestamped response — a time series of signed pings
  • Physics: round trip can't beat the speed of light — hard outer bound
  • Corroborative: authenticated GNSS, camera feeds, satellite imagery, UWB, NFC
  • Anna + Ryan went deep here — the SovCert measurement program
  • [frag 1] E — the evidence: signed location stamps
  • [frag 2] C — the claim under test: declared location + interval
  • [frag 3] concrete output: append-only signed evidence logs
  • A receipt doesn't point at a location — it erases everything outside a circle
  • Assessment = intersection of many circles
  • Per-anchor bundling = redundancy discounting — same path never counted twice
  • Q travels with the map: prior, interval, anchor honesty ε, attester posture, forgery cost
  • Bar: pairwise verification — sufficient to convince a skeptical verifier
  • [frag 1] the evidence function: claim + evidence → assessment
  • [frag 2] classic Bayes: posterior ∝ likelihood × prior
  • P(E|x): how plausible this evidence would be, had the machine been at x
  • (pocket: P(E|x) factorizes across anchors — per-anchor bundling, redundancy discounting)
  • [frag 3] concrete output: the location credibility assessment (π, Q)
  • → FLIP TO THE VIZ TAB HERE — watch belief update receipt by receipt, ~60s
  • Sample sets of pings from the evidence logs; now ask policy questions
  • Containment: P(inside authorized zone) = posterior mass in the polygon
  • Change detection: has it moved? (time series) · Co-location: same data center?
  • Absence of evidence → flag; cryptographic authorization certificates lapse
  • [frag 1] containment: π is mass per grid cell over interval T, sums to 1
  • zone = union of disjoint cells → P(inside) is exactly the sum of their masses
  • continuous intuition: area under the posterior within the geofence — same object
  • [frag 2] verdict = indicator; τ is a policy judgment (false accusation vs missed evasion) — deliberately outside evidence evaluation
  • [frag 3] concrete output: boolean or probability — no more
  • (pocket: boundary cells = implementation choice; result conditional on Q)
  • ZK "extremely feasible" (Pascal) · MPC likely feasible (Pascal, Jack Cheng) — open problems
