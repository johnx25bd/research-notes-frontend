AS-002: Remove inflated delay to conceal diversion

Enrollment, calibration or operation

Add delay

→
Diversion

Attacker moves GPU

→
Concealment

Removes delay; GPU appears unmoved

Could the observations still look acceptable?

  • A seeded hypothesis to examine together, not a demonstrated attack
  • Operator adds delay during enrollment or operation, then removes it (or reduces processing delay) to offset a longer path after diversion
  • Assumes the ability to delay/relay messages and change host/client configuration
  • Does not assume forged GPU signatures
  • Valid responses and similar timing might be observed, but continuity gaps could still reveal the move
  • Whether it works depends on conditions not yet established
  • Ask: how much delay could actually be removed?
  • Ask: what independent reference or continuity evidence would remain?
  • Ask: how would we distinguish this from congestion clearing or an in-place software optimization?
  • Ask: what experiment or analysis would most change our view?
  • ~3 min introduce, 12 discuss, 5 record the decisive uncertainty
  • Reference: attack-scenarios.md#as-002 and its linked investigations; revisions go there

AS-005: Abuse verifier access to seek operator secrets

Verifier

Malicious or compromised

→
Interfaces

Misuses collection, diagnostics or updates

→
Outcome

Operator information beyond what was agreed

Could verifier access reach beyond the agreed evidence?

  • Reverse the adversarial perspective: the verifier as attacker
  • Consider handing to Naci here for practical context
  • First establish whether those interfaces and privileges actually exist in the proposed design
  • Do not assume arbitrary host access
  • Do not imply weights, prompts or other secrets have been shown to be accessible
  • Purpose: inspect the privilege and data boundaries
  • Comparison case: legitimate, bounded collection and diagnostics
  • Ask: what access does the verifier actually have?
  • Ask: what limits collection to the agreed fields and purposes?
  • Ask: who authorizes diagnostics and updates?
  • Ask: what can the operator inspect or restrict while retaining useful verification?
  • ~3 min introduce, 12 discuss, 5 record the decisive uncertainty
  • Reference: attack-scenarios.md#as-005 and its linked investigation
